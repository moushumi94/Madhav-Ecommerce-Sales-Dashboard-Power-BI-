## 📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Madhav Ecommerce sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, profit trends, and category-wise insights. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making through dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.


## 🚀 Key Features
- Sales & profit performance analysis
- Monthly profit trend tracking
- State-wise sales distribution
- Category & sub-category performance analysis
- Customer-wise sales insights
- Payment mode analysis
- Quarterly filtering and interactive slicers
- KPI cards for Amount, Profit, Quantity, and AOV
  

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI  
- DAX  
- Power Query  
- Microsoft Excel  

## 📂 Data Source
- Ecommerce sales dataset collected from Kaggle 

## 📈 Key Insights
- Maharashtra generated the highest sales among all states.
- Clothing category contributed the highest order quantity.
- COD was the most preferred payment method.
- Printers and Bookcases generated higher profits among sub-categories.
- Profit showed strong growth during January and December.


## 💡 Business Impact
- Helped identify high-performing categories and profitable products.
- Enabled analysis of customer purchasing patterns and payment preferences.
- Improved visibility into monthly profit fluctuations for better business planning.
- Provided a centralized dashboard for faster reporting and decision-making.
